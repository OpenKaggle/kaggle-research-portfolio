# Data boundary

This portfolio preserves source code, documentation, and compact research
receipts. It does not redistribute organizer-provided competition data,
episode/replay payloads, record-level splits, predictions, checkpoints, or
third-party notebook copies without a clear permission.

For Kaggriculture, keep episode logs, action/state tapes, and other game
replays in an ignored local working directory. Publish the agent source,
evaluation procedure, aggregate metrics, and a hash manifest instead. For
BioHub, obtain data through the official competition route and keep train/test
membership and prediction metadata outside the public tree.

Before any new research receipt is committed, remove record identifiers,
individual inputs/outputs, raw traces, local paths, and credentials. If bytes
cannot be redistributed, document their official source, expected layout,
version, and checksum rather than copying them here.
