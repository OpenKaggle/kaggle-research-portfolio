## Data and leakage policy

- Official manifest: 24,886 files, 87,609,892,618 bytes. Local free space at campaign start was about 35 GiB, so full local download is intentionally disallowed.
- The four visible test movies are copies of training samples. They may be used only for schema/runtime plumbing, never to configure hidden-test behavior or claim held-out performance.
- Training and hidden test are embryo-disjoint. Validation is grouped by the embryo prefix before the first underscore. Public training currently contains only embryo prefixes `44b6` and `6bba`, so the honest coarse check is leave-one-embryo-out in both directions; within-embryo random folds are diagnostic only and may not be used for promotion.
- No manual labeling or human prediction of validation/test records. No hidden labels, scorer exploits, fake graph forks, or test-specific lookup logic.
- No private code or data sharing outside the Kaggle team. Raw competition data stays local or in Kaggle competition storage even where the license is permissive; the stricter competition data-security clause controls operations.
- Every external artifact must have a public URL, license receipt, version and SHA-256 before use.

## Experiment and submission gates

Every candidate must pass all four gates before submission:

1. Schema: exact ten columns, contiguous row IDs, all hidden datasets covered, valid node/edge sentinels, coordinates in range, and no dangling edges.
2. Leakage: embryo-isolated validation or a sealed holdout; no visible-test tuning and no inaccessible/private artifact.
3. Stability: fixed seeds and hashes, topology constraints, runtime projection below 12 hours, and no material degradation across embryo folds.
4. Rules: Notebook-only, internet off, freely accessible dependencies, documented provenance and no metric exploit.

One variable changes per experiment. Record data version, sources/licenses, code hash, seeds, runtime, offline metric, Kaggle version/submission ID and leaderboard score. Two consecutive failures trigger a method-family change. Three repeats of the same blocker trigger an evidence-backed escalation to the research task.
